# CarryGo Business Model

## Customer economics

The customer can pay for:
- runner execution;
- faster/cheaper negotiated ETA;
- optional same-gender room delivery premium;
- transparent CarryGo service fee.

## Runner economics

The runner earns:
- agreed runner fee;
- room-delivery premium when applicable;
- less CarryGo's configured runner commission.

Runner item reimbursement is separate from runner earnings.

## Discounts

Customer streak discounts reduce CarryGo's service fee. They do not subsidize groceries or runner compensation.

## Merchant economics

Merchants can pay through:
- completed-order fees;
- Merchant Pro software subscription;
- sponsored discovery;
- sponsored streak weeks.

## Institutional/business accounts

Departments, associations, event organizers and student businesses can use bulk-task workflows. This creates a B2B path without turning the consumer app into a corporate procurement UI.

## Cost discipline

Keep these near zero until demand proves them:
- paid maps;
- paid analytics;
- paid CRM;
- dedicated infrastructure;
- large advertising spend.

The core campus graph should let CarryGo answer basic route and ETA questions without paying for a mapping call on every interaction.
