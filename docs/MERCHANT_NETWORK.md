# CarryGo Merchant Network

## Wedge

CarryGo starts with food, groceries and campus essentials because they produce frequent, repeatable execution demand.

Known network:
- Student Central Cafeteria
- Echo-Fresh
- Ngozika
- Omega
- Nabiss
- S.S Mawato
- Munch Box
- Ultimate
- JustEat
- Melons Pack
- Green Plaza

Prices are not assumed to be uniform. CarryGo lets customers describe what they want and optionally lets merchants publish a catalogue.

## Merchant lifecycle

Apply → business verification → location confirmation → storefront → catalogue (optional) → order queue → runner pickup → completion → reconciliation → analytics.

## Launchpad

CarryGo Launchpad is the student/startup merchant package:
- storefront;
- product catalogue;
- runner network;
- basic order queue;
- lightweight demand signals;
- later: analytics and repeat-customer tooling.

Avoid a large upfront “rent” charge for student founders. Monetize through a combination of completed-order fees, optional Merchant Pro tooling and clearly-labelled sponsored discovery.

## Revenue layers

1. Marketplace service fees.
2. Merchant transaction fees.
3. Merchant Pro subscription.
4. Sponsored discovery.
5. Merchant-sponsored streak campaigns.
6. Optional Campus Pass for heavy users.
7. Organization/bulk-task accounts.
8. Later: merchant operations software.

## Guardrails

Sponsored placement cannot hide safety or reliability information. Revenue must not depend on increasing runner friction or quietly taxing item capital.
